Support

We're here to help with our apps, your account, billing, and bug reports.

Fastest path: email support@abappdev.in with the app name, your device and OS version, and a short description of the issue. Most replies go out within 1 business day.

Get help

  • General support: support@abappdev.in

  • Privacy & data requests: privacy@abappdev.in
  • Billing & refunds: billing@abappdev.in
  • Security disclosure: security@abappdev.in

Subscription & refunds

For purchases made on the App Store or Google Play, please use the platform's built-in support flow:

  • Apple — Request a refund or open Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions.
  • Google Play — Refund a Play purchase or open Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

For purchases billed by Stripe on our website, contact billing@abappdev.in with your order ID. Note that charges are non-refundable except where required by law — see our Cancellation & Refund Policy.

Reporting a bug

To help us reproduce a problem quickly, please include:

  • App name and version (Settings → About in most apps)
  • Device model and OS version
  • Steps to reproduce
  • What you expected vs. what happened
  • Screenshots or a screen recording, if you can share one
