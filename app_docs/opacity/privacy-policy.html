Privacy Policy

For the Opacity Social mobile application.

Effective Date: May 29, 2026 Last Updated: May 29, 2026 Publisher: AB App Dev

This notice covers the Opacity Social Android app. It supplements, and where it conflicts controls over, the publisher-wide AB App Dev Privacy Policy.

1. What Opacity Social does

Opacity Social is a project management and team collaboration app. It supports projects, chats, invites, and Kanban boards. To deliver these features the app needs to handle some personal data on your behalf.

2. Data we collect

CategoryExamplesPurpose
Account dataDisplay name, email address, profile photo, authentication identifiersCreate and protect your account

We do not request or store full payment card numbers. Payments are processed by the platform store you used (Apple or Google) or by Stripe for any web purchases.

3. Permissions we may request

  • Internet / network: required to sync your data and send messages.
  • Notifications: optional, used to alert you to invites, mentions, and updates.
  • Camera and photo library: optional, used only when you choose to attach an image or set a profile photo.
  • Storage: used to cache content for offline use.

Permissions are requested when a feature needs them, and you can revoke them in your device settings at any time.

4. How we use your data

  • Provide the core collaboration features (projects, chats, invites, Kanban).
  • Authenticate you and keep your workspace secure.
  • Send service notifications you opt into.
  • Diagnose crashes, prevent abuse, and improve quality.
  • Process subscriptions and comply with tax and accounting obligations.
  • Respond to support requests.

5. Sharing

We share data only with processors that help us run the app, such as cloud storage and database providers (for example, Google Firebase / Firestore), authentication providers, crash reporting, push notification gateways, and payment processors (Apple, Google, Stripe). We do not sell or rent personal information and do not use it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Other members of a workspace will see content you post in shared projects, chats, and boards. If you invite a teammate, the invitation contains the contact identifier you provided.

6. Payments

If you purchase a subscription through Google Play or the App Store, the platform handles the transaction under its terms and privacy policy. If you purchase through our website, Stripe handles the transaction under Stripe’s Privacy Policy. We receive only a transaction confirmation, an opaque customer/charge identifier, the amount and currency, the country of purchase, and limited card metadata (such as brand and last four digits) for receipts and dispute handling.

7. Data retention

We keep account and workspace data while your account is active. Crash and diagnostic logs are typically retained for up to 90 days. Transaction records may be retained for up to 7–10 years where required by tax or accounting law. When you delete your account, your personal data is removed or anonymized within 30 days, except where we must retain certain records by law or to resolve disputes.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest by our cloud provider. Access to production systems is restricted, audited, and limited to least-privilege admins. To report a vulnerability, please email security@abappdev.in.

9. International transfers

Our processors may store and process data outside your country, including in the United States and the European Union. Where required, transfers are protected by Standard Contractual Clauses or comparable safeguards.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, port, restrict, or delete your data, to object to certain processing, to withdraw consent, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (such as your local Data Protection Authority, the UK ICO, or India’s Data Protection Board under the DPDP Act, 2023). To exercise any right, email privacy@abappdev.in. We do not sell or share personal information as defined under the CCPA/CPRA.

11. Children

Opacity Social is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact privacy@abappdev.in and we will delete it.

12. Changes

We may update this notice. Material changes will be flagged in-app or here. The “Last Updated” date reflects the most recent revision.
