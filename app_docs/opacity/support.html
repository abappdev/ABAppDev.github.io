Opacity Social Support

Help with projects, chats, invites, Kanban boards, and your subscription.

Email us at support@abappdev.in with the app version (Settings → About), your device model, and a short description of the issue. Most replies go out within 1 business day.

Common questions

How do I invite someone to a project?

Open the project, tap the invite icon, and enter the person’s email or username. They will receive an invitation they can accept inside the app.

Why am I not getting notifications?

Make sure notifications are enabled for Opacity Social in your device settings, that the app has run at least once after install, and that you’re signed in. Some Android devices also need the app exempt from battery optimization.

How do I move a card on the Kanban board?

Long-press a card and drag it to another column, or open the card and change its status from the menu.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings → Account → Delete account in the app, or email privacy@abappdev.in. Your personal data is removed or anonymized within 30 days, except where law requires us to keep limited records.

Subscriptions, billing, and refunds

  • Google Play: manage your plan in Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Refunds: Google Play refund help.
  • App Store (if available): manage in Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions. Refunds: Apple billing help.
  • Web (Stripe): contact billing@abappdev.in with your order ID. Our refund policy is in the Terms & Conditions.

Report a bug

Email bugs@abappdev.in and please include:

  • App version (Settings → About)
  • Device model and OS version
  • Steps to reproduce
  • What you expected vs. what happened
  • Screenshots or a screen recording, if possible

Feature requests

We’d love to hear what would make Opacity Social better for your team. Email feedback@abappdev.in.

Security

To report a security issue privately, please email security@abappdev.in. We take responsible disclosure seriously and will respond as quickly as we can.
